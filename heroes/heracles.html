THE HERO

Heracles

Heracles, known to the Romans as Hercules, was one of the most famous heroes of Greek mythology. He was renowned for extraordinary strength, courage and endurance.

He was traditionally described as the son of Zeus and the mortal woman Alcmene. His life was marked by immense challenges, including the famous Twelve Labours.

THE TWELVE LABOURS

His Greatest Trials

The Twelve Labours became the defining achievement of Heracles. Among them were defeating the Nemean Lion, destroying the Lernaean Hydra, capturing the Ceryneian Hind, obtaining the Golden Apples of the Hesperides and capturing Cerberus.

Each Labour represented a seemingly impossible challenge, requiring strength, courage, cleverness or endurance.

“The impossible became the measure of his strength.”

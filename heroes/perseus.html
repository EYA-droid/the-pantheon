THE HERO

Perseus

Perseus was one of the great heroes of Greek mythology and was traditionally described as the son of Zeus and Danaë.

His most famous achievement was defeating Medusa, the Gorgon whose gaze was said to turn living beings to stone.

THE QUEST

The Gorgon

Perseus received divine assistance for his quest. By using a reflective shield rather than looking directly at Medusa, he was able to approach her safely.

Afterward, his adventures continued, including the rescue of Andromeda from a sea monster.

“Courage sometimes begins by refusing to look directly into danger.”

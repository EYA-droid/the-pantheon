THE HUNTRESS

Atalanta

Atalanta was a legendary heroine renowned for her speed, hunting ability and independence.

She is associated with the Calydonian Boar hunt and the famous footrace in which golden apples played a crucial role.

THE RACE

Golden Apples

Atalanta agreed to marry only a man who could defeat her in a race. Hippomenes used golden apples to distract her during the contest.

“Speed, skill and independence made her unlike any other hero.”

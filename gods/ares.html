ARES

God of War · Battle · Conflict · The Fury of Combat

THE EXHIBIT

The God of War

Ares represents the violent and chaotic side of warfare. He is associated with battle, conflict, courage and the fury of combat.

Unlike Athena, whose warfare is associated with strategy and intelligence, Ares represents the raw force and danger of battle itself.

DOMAIN War · Conflict · Battle
PARENTS Zeus & Hera
SYMBOLS Spear · Shield · Helmet
ARES & ATHENA

Two Faces of War

Greek mythology often places Ares and Athena in contrast. Both are connected with warfare, but they represent different aspects of it.

Athena embodies planning, strategy and disciplined action. Ares embodies aggression, violence and the chaos of combat.

War requires both the mind that plans and the force that fights.

APHRODITE

Ares & Aphrodite

Ares was famously connected with Aphrodite, the goddess of love and beauty.

Their relationship created one of Greek mythology's most striking contrasts: war and love existing side by side.

The stories surrounding them became popular subjects in ancient art and later European literature.

THE WARRIOR

A Dangerous Reputation

Ares was not always portrayed as a heroic warrior. Greek myths often depict him as impulsive and destructive.

Even other gods could oppose him, demonstrating that physical power alone did not make someone a respected leader.

LEGACY

Ares represents war without the illusion that battle is glorious.

Through Ares, Greek mythology personified the fear, anger and unpredictability of conflict.

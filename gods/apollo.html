APOLLO

God of Music · Prophecy · Healing · Light

THE EXHIBIT

The Golden God

Apollo is one of the most influential gods in Greek mythology. He is associated with music, poetry, prophecy, healing and archery.

Apollo represents many forms of order, beauty and knowledge. His sanctuary at Delphi became one of the most important religious centres of the ancient Greek world.

DOMAIN Music · Prophecy · Healing
PARENTS Zeus & Leto
SIBLING Artemis
DELPHI

Voice of Prophecy

At Delphi, Apollo was connected with the famous oracle. The priestess known as the Pythia delivered prophetic messages that were consulted by people from across the Greek world.

Kings, travellers and ordinary people could seek guidance about important decisions and uncertain futures.

DAPHNE

The Laurel Tree

One famous story tells of Apollo and Daphne. Daphne, attempting to escape Apollo, was transformed into a laurel tree.

Apollo then made the laurel sacred to himself. The story became an important explanation for the connection between Apollo and the laurel.

LEGACY

Apollo stands at the crossroads of music, knowledge, prophecy and divine order.

His influence reaches from ancient temples and prophetic sanctuaries to poetry, music and art that continue to use his image today.

HEPHAESTUS

God of Fire · Metalworking · Smithing · Craftsmanship

THE EXHIBIT

Master of the Forge

Hephaestus is the divine smith of Greek mythology, associated with fire, metalworking, craftsmanship and invention.

His forge produces extraordinary weapons, armour and objects for the gods. He represents skill, creativity and the power of making.

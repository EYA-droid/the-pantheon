THE EXHIBIT

The Goddess of Love

Aphrodite is the Greek goddess associated with love, beauty, desire and attraction. She is one of the most famous figures in Greek mythology.

Her power was not limited to romantic love. The Greeks also connected her with beauty, desire, fertility and the irresistible forces that bring people together.

DOMAIN Love · Beauty · Desire
ASSOCIATED WITH Sea · Roses · Doves
PARTNER Hephaestus · Ares in myth
BIRTH

Born From the Sea

One famous tradition says Aphrodite emerged from the sea foam and arrived upon the shore.

This dramatic origin became one of the most enduring images in Greek mythology and later inspired countless artists.

From the sea came the goddess whose beauty could influence gods and mortals alike.

THE GOLDEN APPLE

The Judgement of Paris

At the wedding of Peleus and Thetis, the goddess Eris caused trouble by introducing a golden apple intended for the fairest goddess.

Hera, Athena and Aphrodite each claimed it. Paris of Troy was chosen to decide which goddess was the fairest.

Aphrodite promised Paris the love of the most beautiful woman in the world. Paris selected Aphrodite, a decision that helped set the events leading to the Trojan War in motion.

ARES

Love & War

Aphrodite's relationship with Ares creates one of Greek mythology's most memorable contrasts: love and war.

The two gods appear together in several myths and became a powerful symbolic pairing in ancient storytelling.

LEGACY

Aphrodite represents the powerful forces of attraction, beauty and desire.

Her image has survived for thousands of years, appearing again and again in sculpture, painting, poetry and literature.

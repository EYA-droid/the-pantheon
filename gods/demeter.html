DEMETER

Goddess of Agriculture · Harvest · Fertility · The Earth

THE EXHIBIT

Mother of the Harvest

Demeter is the goddess of agriculture, grain and the harvest. Her mythology is deeply connected with the cycles of nature.

Her story with Persephone became one of the central explanations for seasonal change in Greek mythology.

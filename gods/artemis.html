HALL OF OLYMPUS · EXHIBIT VII

ARTEMIS

Goddess of the Hunt · Wilderness · Archery · The Moon

THE EXHIBIT

Lady of the Wild

Artemis is the Greek goddess associated with the hunt, wilderness, wild animals and archery. She is also connected with childbirth and the protection of young people.

She represents the untamed world beyond the walls of cities: forests, mountains, animals and the freedom of the hunt.

DOMAIN Hunt · Wilderness · Archery
PARENTS Zeus & Leto
SIBLING Apollo
THE HUNTRESS

Mistress of the Bow

Artemis is commonly portrayed carrying a bow and travelling through wild landscapes with a group of hunting companions.

Her skill with the bow reflects her independence and her close relationship with the wilderness.

Beyond the walls of the city lies the realm of Artemis.

ACTAEON

A Warning to Mortals

One famous myth tells of Actaeon, a hunter who encountered Artemis while she was bathing.

The myth illustrates the strict boundaries surrounding the goddess and the dangers of violating sacred spaces.

TWIN OF APOLLO

Artemis & Apollo

Artemis and Apollo are twins, children of Zeus and Leto. Their domains often complement one another.

Apollo became associated with music, prophecy and healing, while Artemis became associated with the wilderness, hunting and the natural world.

FAMILY

The House of Artemis

FATHER Zeus
MOTHER Leto
TWIN BROTHER Apollo
LEGACY

Artemis embodies the freedom, beauty and danger of the world beyond civilisation.

Her mythology reminds us that the ancient Greeks saw the wilderness as a place filled with both wonder and divine power.

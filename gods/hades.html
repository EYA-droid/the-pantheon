HADES

Lord of the Underworld · Keeper of the Dead · God of Hidden Wealth

THE EXHIBIT

Lord Beneath the Earth

Hades is the ruler of the Underworld, the realm of the dead in Greek mythology. He is one of the three brothers who divided the cosmos after the defeat of the Titans.

While Zeus received the sky and Poseidon received the sea, Hades received dominion over the world beneath the earth. His realm was not simply a place of punishment — it was the destination of the dead.

Hades is therefore better understood as a stern ruler who maintains the boundaries between life and death.

DOMAIN Underworld · The Dead · Wealth
PARENTS Cronus & Rhea
SYMBOLS Bident · Cerberus · Helm of Darkness
THE REALM BELOW

The Underworld

The Greek Underworld was imagined as a vast and complex realm. Different regions were associated with different destinies for the dead.

🌊

Styx

⚖️

🌿

Elysium

A blessed destination associated with exceptional souls in later traditions.

🔥

Tartarus

A deep region associated with imprisonment and punishment.

GUARDIAN OF THE GATES

Cerberus

Cerberus is the legendary guardian of the entrance to the Underworld. The monstrous hound prevents the dead from leaving and the living from entering freely.

The creature became one of the most famous beings connected with Hades and the Underworld.

No traveller simply walks into the realm of the dead.
THE QUEEN BELOW

Hades & Persephone

One of the best-known myths connected with Hades concerns Persephone, daughter of Demeter.

The myth describes Persephone's movement between the world above and the Underworld. Her return to the surface became connected with the growth of vegetation and the changing seasons.

The story became one of the most important myths associated with the ancient Greek understanding of life, death and renewal.

LEGACY

Hades is not simply the god of death. He is the ruler of the world beyond life.

His mythology reflects one of the oldest human questions: what happens when life ends?

Through the Underworld, the Greeks created a vast mythological landscape where the dead continued their existence under the authority of a powerful and impartial king.

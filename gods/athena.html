ATHENA

Goddess of Wisdom · Strategic Warfare · Crafts · Guardian of Athens

THE EXHIBIT

The Wise Goddess

Athena is one of the most important Olympian gods. She represents wisdom, strategic warfare, crafts and reason.

Unlike Ares, who represents the violent and chaotic side of warfare, Athena is associated with planning, discipline, strategy and intelligent decision-making.

She became the patron deity of Athens, one of the most important cities of the ancient Greek world.

DOMAIN Wisdom · Strategy · Crafts
FATHER Zeus
SYMBOLS Owl · Olive Tree · Aegis
BIRTH OF ATHENA

Born From Zeus

One of the strangest birth stories in Greek mythology says that Athena emerged fully grown and armed from the head of Zeus.

The story emphasises her connection with intelligence, thought and divine wisdom.

Athena did not simply possess wisdom — she was born from the mind of Zeus.

ATHENS

The Contest for the City

Athena and Poseidon competed for the patronage of the city that would eventually become Athens.

Poseidon offered a powerful gift, while Athena offered the olive tree. The olive tree was valued for its food, oil, wood and many uses.

Athena became the city's patron and the Parthenon was later dedicated to her.

HEROES

Athena & Odysseus

Athena frequently appears as a supporter of heroes who demonstrate intelligence and courage.

She is especially important in the story of Odysseus, whose cleverness and strategic thinking made him one of her favourite mortal heroes.

LEGACY

Athena represents the power of a mind that can turn knowledge into action.

Her mythology connects wisdom with courage, strategy, craftsmanship and civilisation.

POSEIDON

Lord of the Sea · Earth-Shaker · Master of Horses

THE EXHIBIT

Lord of the Sea

Poseidon is one of the great Olympian gods and ruler of the sea. He is also associated with earthquakes and horses.

His power extends across the oceans and coastal lands of the Greek world. Sailors respected him and feared his unpredictable anger.

DOMAIN Sea · Earthquakes · Horses
PARENTS Cronus & Rhea
SYMBOL The Trident
THE TRIDENT

Weapon of the Sea

Poseidon's three-pronged trident is one of the most recognisable objects in Greek mythology.

With it, Poseidon could stir the sea, cause earthquakes and demonstrate his immense divine power.

LEGACY

The sea can give life, carry travellers across the world, or unleash forces powerful enough to reshape the earth.

That dual nature is central to Poseidon's character in Greek mythology.

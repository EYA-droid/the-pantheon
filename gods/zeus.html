ZEUS

King of the Gods · Lord of the Sky · Wielder of the Thunderbolt

THE EXHIBIT

King of the Gods

Zeus is the principal ruler of the Olympian gods in Greek mythology. He is associated with the sky, thunder, justice, hospitality and kingship.

In many traditions, Zeus became ruler after leading the younger generation of gods against the Titans. From Mount Olympus, he was regarded as a powerful guardian of divine and cosmic order.

DOMAIN Sky · Thunder · Kingship
PARENTS Cronus & Rhea
SYMBOLS Thunderbolt · Eagle · Oak
ORIGINS

The Rise of Zeus

Zeus was the youngest child of Cronus and Rhea. Cronus feared a prophecy that one of his children would overthrow him, so he swallowed his children when they were born.

Rhea protected Zeus by hiding him and giving Cronus a stone wrapped in cloth instead. Zeus eventually returned, helped free his siblings and led the younger gods in the struggle against the Titans.

After victory, the realms were divided among the brothers Zeus, Poseidon and Hades.

LEGACY

Zeus stands at the centre of the Olympian order, representing power, authority and the unpredictable forces of the sky.

His stories appear throughout Greek mythology and connect him to gods, heroes, kings and cities across the ancient Greek world.

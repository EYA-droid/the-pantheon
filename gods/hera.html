HERA

Queen of the Gods · Goddess of Marriage · Guardian of the Family

THE EXHIBIT

Queen of Olympus

Hera is the queen of the Olympian gods and is especially associated with marriage, women, family and royal authority.

She is both the sister and wife of Zeus. Greek myths frequently explore her powerful personality, her authority among the gods, and the conflicts created by Zeus's many relationships.

DOMAINMarriage · Queenship · Family
PARENTSCronus & Rhea
SYMBOLSPeacock · Diadem · Pomegranate
HER ROLE

The Divine Queen

Hera represents the institution of marriage and the authority of a divine queen. Her position makes her one of the most important figures on Mount Olympus.

Although many myths portray Hera in conflict with Zeus and those connected to him, these stories also demonstrate her power, intelligence and determination.

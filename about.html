About Greek Mythology

Stories preserved through poetry, art, religion, and tradition.

The world of Greek mythology

A tradition of gods, heroes, monsters, and mortals.

Greek mythology is a collection of ancient stories about gods, heroes, monsters, and mortals. These stories developed over many centuries and were passed through oral traditions, religious practices, poetry, drama, visual art, and later written works.

Greek myths explored questions about the natural world, human behavior, divine power, family, justice, pride, love, suffering, and fate. Different authors and regions sometimes preserved different versions of the same story.

How the myths survived

Homeric Tradition

The works traditionally attributed to Homer preserve major traditions about the Trojan War and its aftermath.

Hesiod

Theogony presents a major account of divine genealogy and the succession of cosmic powers.

Greek Drama

Greek tragedians adapted myths into dramatic stories that remain influential today.
